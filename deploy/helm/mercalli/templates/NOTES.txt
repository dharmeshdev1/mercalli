Mercalli is starting. It is ready once it reaches the database and finishes its first poll of
every status page, usually within a minute or two.

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deployment/{{ include "mercalli.fullname" . }}
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ include "mercalli.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Then open http://localhost:8080 for the web app, or:

  curl -s localhost:8080/readyz
  curl -s localhost:8080/api/v1/overview.json
  curl -sN localhost:8080/api/v1/stream
